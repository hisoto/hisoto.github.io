---
title: "Age-Period-Cohort Effects on Labor Market Trends in Mexico"
excerpt: "Decomposing Mexican labor market trends into age, cohort, and period effects using ENOE pseudo-panels (2005–2025).<br/>"

---

This project replicates and extends the analysis of Duval-Hernández & Orraca Romano (2009) using data from the ENOE (Encuesta Nacional de Ocupación y Empleo) spanning 2005–2025. The objective is to decompose labor market trends in Mexico into three components:

- **Age effect** — changes over the individual life cycle.
- **Cohort effect** — persistent generational differences between groups born in different years.
- **Period effect** — cyclical fluctuations associated with the macroeconomic environment.

The estimation follows Deaton's (1997) method: a weighted least squares (WLS) model in log-odds, with full dummies for age, cohort, and period, where the period effect is constrained to be orthogonal to a linear trend. Cohorts are defined by birth year, education level (basic, intermediate, higher), and gender.

**Variables of interest:** labor force participation rate, unemployment rate, and employment shares in the formal sector, informal wage employment, and self-employment.
